70-year-old tradition lives on Katak Dussehra:

Puppets that tell Cuttack's story Unlike traditional Indian puppetry, which primarily draws on mythological epics such as the Ramayana and Mahabharata, Dhinki Kuta depicts the daily life and kitchen chores of a typical Odia household.Amid the elaboratedecorations, grand idols and silver tableaux that define Cuttack's Durga Puja celebrations, the traditional puppet display at Chhatra Bazar stands out as a reminder of the city's rich cultural heritage. It is home to a 70-year-old puppet tradition known as Dhinki Kuta, a unique and rare form of puppetry staged annually during Durga Puja.
